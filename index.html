HOW TO PLAY

  • ◀ ▶ (or ← →, or tilt your phone) to steer — 2969 bounces on his own.
  • Land on the ledges to climb. Off the sides you wrap around.
  • Grab $OIL cans to fill the gauge — a full gauge bounces you over a ledge.
  • Springs: land right on the coil to fly ~6 ledges. Hover pads slide; rusty plates crumble after one bounce.
  • Combo: keep landing 2+ ledges up, each within 3 seconds — a combo of 4 ledges or more pays ledges × ledges.
  • The lava wakes at ledge 5 and climbs faster the higher you go. One touch ends the run.
  • A new world every 100 ledges. Climb as high as you can.
